Grammar quick reference

Current working rules.

Sentence order: SOVSubject – Object – Verb: “I the truth know.”
Plural: -riAdd -ri to a noun to make it plural.
Possession: naPossessor + na + noun; “of” or belonging to.
Negation: nuPlace nu before the verb.
Past: taPlace ta before the verb.
Future: ruPlace ru before the verb.
Questions: eiPlace ei at the start of a question.
